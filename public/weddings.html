Wedding Questions

Planning Your Day

Can we host our entire wedding at Villa Azure Hotel?

Yes. Reserve the full hotel for a private buyout — an oceanfront ceremony in the gardens, a reception under the open-air glass pavilion, and all sixteen suites reserved exclusively for your party.

How many guests can stay on-site?

Up to 38 guests stay overnight across the hotel's sixteen en-suite rooms and suites — so your closest family and friends can celebrate and sleep on the property.

Where does the ceremony take place?

On the property — in the oceanfront gardens and the open-air glass pavilion, with the Atlantic as your backdrop.

Do you provide catering?

Our in-house private chef creates custom wedding menus, from cocktail hour to a seated multi-course dinner.

Is there a covered option if it rains?

Yes — the open-air glass pavilion and the indoor coastal dining room provide beautiful covered spaces for your ceremony or reception.

How do we reserve a date?

Send us your date and guest count and our team will prepare a personalized quote and hold your date.
