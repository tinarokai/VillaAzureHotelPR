Trade Winds Included

One of Puerto Rico's great kite beaches

Ocean Park is widely regarded as one of the best kitesurfing spots in Puerto Rico. Steady Atlantic trade winds, a long open stretch of sand and water without reef breaks close to shore, and a friendly local scene that launches right off the beach.

Villa Azure sits on that same sand. Wake up, look at the palms to read the wind, and walk out. Our concierge connects you with local schools for lessons, gear rental and guided sessions.

Good to Know

Kitesurfing questions

Is Ocean Park really a good kitesurfing spot?

Yes. Ocean Park Beach is widely regarded as one of the best kiteboarding spots in Puerto Rico, with steady Atlantic trade winds and launches right off the sand.

Can beginners learn here?

Absolutely. Local schools on and around Ocean Park Beach offer beginner lessons and rental gear; our concierge will connect you.

Does the hotel rent kite equipment?

We don't rent gear ourselves, but lessons and rentals with trusted local schools are steps away and easy to arrange through the concierge.
