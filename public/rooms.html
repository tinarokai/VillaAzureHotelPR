Villa Azure Hotel, where you can immerse yourself in a serene and rejuvenating experience.

Experience the ultimate in comfort and luxury at Villa Azure Hotel. We offer sixteen exquisite suites, each with its own private bathroom featuring rain showers and soaking tubs, high-definition smart TVs, high-speed Wi-Fi, a curated minibar, and a Nespresso coffee machine to ensure your convenience.

Our rooms are thoughtfully designed with modern amenities and a Caribbean touch, showcasing luxurious linens and bespoke furnishings. When you stay with us, you’ll have access to our stunning pool and communal areas, enhancing your overall experience. Villa Azure Hotel is more than just a place to stay; it’s an invitation to relax, explore, and create lasting memories in the heart of San Juan.
