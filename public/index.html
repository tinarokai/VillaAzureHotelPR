Good to Know

Frequently asked

Where is Villa Azure Hotel located?

At 1 Calle Guerrero Noble in Ocean Park, San Juan, steps from Ocean Park Beach and minutes from Condado and Old San Juan.

How many guests can the hotel accommodate?

Sixteen en-suite suites, many with ocean views, sleeping up to 38 guests. Reserve a suite, a full floor, or the entire hotel for your group.

Is Villa Azure Hotel on the beach?

Yes, the hotel is beachfront in Ocean Park with direct beach access and two pools, just steps from the sand.

Do you offer a private chef and concierge?

Yes. Our in-house private chef prepares everything from poolside breakfast to oceanfront dinners, and our concierge arranges tours, charters, transfers and tailored itineraries.

Can I host a wedding or corporate event here?

Absolutely. With oceanfront event spaces, sixteen suites and full-hotel buyouts, Villa Azure is ideal for weddings, corporate retreats and group celebrations.

How do I book?

Book directly through our online booking engine or via WhatsApp, with no OTA fees when you book direct.
