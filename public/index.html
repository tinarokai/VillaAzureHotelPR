Villa Azure Hotel

Welcome to Villa Azure Hotel, a refined boutique retreat where elegance and serenity meet. Set on the sand in the heart of Ocean Park, this oceanfront haven offers an intimate and sophisticated experience, blending timeless luxury with modern comfort — and the ease of a private residence with the service of a resort.

Indulge in sixteen exquisitely designed suites, each with its own private bath, ensuring privacy and relaxation for up to 32 guests. Unwind in the pools, enjoy the ambiance of stylish living spaces, or prepare a gourmet meal in the fully equipped kitchens.

Designed by acclaimed interior designer Steve Scarett and adorned with bespoke artwork by Federick Valentín, every detail reflects a seamless harmony of art, comfort, and sophistication.

Located steps from Ocean Park Beach, Villa Azure Hotel invites you to embrace stunning sunsets and the vibrant charm of San Juan. Whether for an intimate escape or a grand celebration, this exclusive hideaway is more than a stay—it’s an experience.

Frequently Asked

Good to Know

Where is Villa Azure Hotel located?

Villa Azure Hotel sits at 1 Calle Guerrero Noble in Ocean Park, San Juan, Puerto Rico — steps from Ocean Park Beach and minutes from Condado and Old San Juan.

How many guests can the hotel accommodate?

The hotel offers sixteen en-suite ocean-view suites and sleeps up to 32 guests. Reserve a single suite, a full floor, or book the entire hotel for your group.

Is Villa Azure Hotel on the beach?

Yes — the hotel is oceanfront in Ocean Park with direct beach access and two private pools, just steps from the sand.

Do you offer a private chef and concierge?

Yes. Our in-house private chef prepares everything from poolside breakfast to multi-course oceanfront dinners, and our personalized concierge arranges tours, boat charters, transfers and tailored itineraries — often at no extra cost.

Can I host a wedding or corporate event here?

Absolutely. With oceanfront event spaces, sixteen suites and full-hotel buyouts, Villa Azure Hotel is ideal for weddings, corporate retreats and group celebrations in San Juan.

How do I book Villa Azure Hotel?

Book directly through our online booking engine, by phone at 561-289-2565, or via WhatsApp — no OTA fees when you book direct.
