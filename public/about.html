About Us

Where Boutique Luxury Meets Caribbean Charm

Welcome to Villa Azure

Discover the Essence of Luxury at Villa Azure

Nestled in Ocean Park, Villa Azure Boutique Hotel is a sanctuary of refined luxury and exclusivity. Offering sophisticated suites, bespoke amenities, and personalized concierge, it’s the perfect retreat for romantic getaways, family gatherings, or corporate escapes.

Steps from the beach, every detail is designed for an unforgettable ocean retreat experience in Puerto Rico.

Design & Art

Where Every Detail is a Work of Art

Villa Azure is a masterpiece of design and culture, brought to life by two visionary creators. Puerto Rican artist Federick Valentín weaves the essence of the island into striking artistic expressions, while renowned interior designer Steve Carril curates an ambiance of contemporary elegance and refined comfort. Together, they have created a seamless fusion of art and luxury, transforming every space into an immersive visual and sensory experience, where sophistication meets the soul of Puerto Rico.

The Property

An Exclusive Ocean Retreat

Experience the ultimate in privacy and sophistication at Villa Azure Hotel, featuring 16 beautifully appointed suites, each with its own private bathroom, accommodating up to 32 guests across king and queen configurations. Revel in breathtaking ocean views while enjoying premium amenities, including spacious great rooms, fully equipped kitchens, private pools and oceanfront gardens.

Step out onto the balcony, fire up the BBQ in the outdoor area, or unwind in the lush garden. With convenient parking and quick access to the beach, this ocean retreat is the perfect setting for unforgettable gatherings with family and friends.
