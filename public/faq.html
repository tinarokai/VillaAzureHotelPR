Getting Here

Location & Arrival

Where is Villa Azure Hotel located?

At 1 Calle Guerrero Noble in Ocean Park, San Juan, Puerto Rico 00913. The hotel sits beachfront, steps from Ocean Park Beach and minutes from Condado and Old San Juan.

How far is the hotel from the airport?

Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU) is about a 15 minute drive away. Our concierge can arrange private airport transfers for arrivals and departures.

What is the Ocean Park neighborhood like?

Ocean Park is a calm, mostly residential beachfront neighborhood between Condado and Isla Verde. It has one of San Juan's widest and most beautiful beaches, a relaxed local atmosphere, nearby cafes, and it is one of Puerto Rico's best-known kitesurfing spots.

Is parking available?

Yes. Villa Azure Hotel offers free on-site parking for guests, making arrivals and departures easy.

The Property

Suites & Amenities

How many suites are there and how many guests can the hotel sleep?

Yes. The hotel is beachfront in Ocean Park with direct beach access, just steps from the sand.

Do the suites have ocean views?

Many of the sixteen suites have ocean views, and every guest enjoys the beachfront setting, the two pools and the shared terraces. Details for each suite are on the Suites page.

Does the hotel have a pool?

Yes, two private pools: a main pool and a quiet pool with a sun terrace, both surrounded by tropical gardens.

Yes. Fast WiFi is free throughout the hotel, including the suites, pools and common areas.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed at the hotel.

Beyond the Hotel

Visiting Puerto Rico

What are the best things to do in San Juan?

Walk Old San Juan and the El Morro fortress, eat and dance at La Placita de Santurce, spend beach days right on Ocean Park, and day-trip to El Yunque rainforest. Kitesurfing, snorkeling and boat charters are all close by, and our concierge can arrange any of it.

What are the best restaurants near Ocean Park?

Kasalta, the legendary Ocean Park bakery and deli, is a short walk from the hotel, along with beachfront spots like Pamela's and Numero Uno Beach House. Ten minutes away, La Placita de Santurce has standouts like Santaella and Jose Enrique, plus the Lote 23 food park. In Old San Juan, Marmalade is a favorite for a dressed-up dinner.

Which beaches should we visit?

Ocean Park Beach, directly in front of the hotel, is one of San Juan's widest and most relaxed. Condado and Isla Verde are minutes away, Escambrón is the local snorkeling spot, and Flamenco Beach on Culebra, often ranked among the world's best, makes a great day trip.

What day trips can we take from the hotel?

El Yunque rainforest is about 45 minutes east. Kayak the glowing bioluminescent bay in Fajardo at night, ferry or fly to Culebra for Flamenco Beach, visit Vieques and Mosquito Bay, or graze the beachside food kiosks of Piñones just past Isla Verde.

Do I need a passport to visit Puerto Rico?

US citizens do not need a passport. Puerto Rico is a US territory, so domestic flights, US dollars, and US phone plans all work exactly like home. International visitors follow the same entry requirements as for the mainland United States.

When is the best time to visit Puerto Rico?

December through April is the dry, breezy high season. May through November is warmer and quieter with better availability, and the ocean stays swimmable all year, with temperatures around 75 to 85 degrees in every month.

How do we get around San Juan?

Uber is reliable across San Juan, and Ocean Park itself is walkable to the beach, cafes and Kasalta. You only need a rental car for day trips like El Yunque or Fajardo, and the hotel has free parking.

What is the nightlife like near Ocean Park?

La Placita de Santurce, ten minutes away, turns into San Juan's liveliest open-air party from Thursday to Saturday. Condado has lounges and casinos, and Old San Juan mixes historic bars with salsa spots. Ocean Park itself stays quiet at night, which is exactly why guests love sleeping here.
