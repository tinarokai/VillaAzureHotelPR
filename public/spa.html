The Spa Comes to You

Licensed therapists and estheticians, set up wherever you're most comfortable.

Wellness, Delivered

We bring the spa to you

Villa Azure doesn't keep a spa behind a door, and that's the point. Licensed local therapists and estheticians come to the hotel and set up where you're most comfortable: your suite, the garden, or poolside under the palms.

Facials, body treatments, and full pamper days are all arranged by our concierge. Tell us what you'd like and when, and we'll take care of the rest.

At a Glance

How it works

  • Treatments in your suite, the garden, or poolside
  • Facials, body treatments and pamper packages
  • Licensed, vetted local professionals
  • Arranged by our concierge, ideally 24–48 hours ahead
  • Priced per treatment, ask us for the current menu
Good to Know

Spa questions

Is there a spa on the property?

There is no built spa on site. Instead, Villa Azure arranges visiting licensed therapists and estheticians who set up the full experience in your suite, the gardens, or poolside.

How do I book a treatment?

Ask our concierge or message us on WhatsApp. We recommend 24–48 hours of notice to secure your preferred time.

Are treatments included in the room rate?

Treatments are arranged on request and priced separately per service. Ask us for the current menu and rates.
