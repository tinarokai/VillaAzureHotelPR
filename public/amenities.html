Spaces & Experiences

Curated for Your Comfort

From two pools to direct beach access, an in-house chef and personalized concierge — every amenity has been thoughtfully selected for a seamless oceanfront stay.

On Property

Two Private Pools

Two stunning private pools framed by lush tropical landscaping — a glass-edged oceanfront pool and a quiet heated pool with sun terrace. The perfect spots for a refreshing dip or an afternoon of relaxation.

At the Door

Direct Beach Access

Just steps from the pristine sands of Ocean Park Beach, one of San Juan's most beautiful and serene stretches of coastline — the garden opens straight onto the shore.

Alfresco

BBQ Terrace

Gather with family and friends at our fully equipped BBQ terrace, perfect for evening cookouts and long dinners under the Caribbean sky.

Every Suite

Ocean & Pool Views

Breathtaking ocean and pool views from multiple suites and common areas, including sunset-facing terraces and ocean-view balconies.

Culinary

Full Chef's Kitchen

Fully equipped chef's kitchens and kitchenettes with modern appliances, cookware, and everything you need to prepare meals — or hand it over to our in-house chef.

Indoors

Living Spaces

Spacious, stylishly furnished living rooms and lounge areas — hung with original art and designed for relaxation and easy socializing.

By Request

In-House Private Chef

Our in-house chef designs gourmet menus around your tastes — from a relaxed poolside breakfast to an elegant multi-course dinner in the oceanfront dining room.

Anytime

Personalized Concierge

From the moment you inquire, our concierge is at your service — private boat charters, car rentals, airport transfers and guided island tours, arranged seamlessly and often at no extra cost.

Arrival

On-Site Parking

Convenient on-site parking ensures hassle-free arrivals and departures for all our guests.

Connectivity

High-Speed WiFi

Stay connected with complimentary high-speed WiFi throughout the entire property.

In-Room

Smart TVs

Flat-screen smart TVs in every suite with streaming capabilities for your entertainment.

In-Room

Air Conditioning

Modern climate control in every room ensures your comfort throughout your stay.
