Around the Hotel

Curated destinations, just minutes away.

From iconic beaches to colonial fortresses to vibrant nightlife, Ocean Park sits at the center of San Juan’s best.

Ocean Park Beach

One of San Juan’s most beloved beaches: wide shoreline, relaxed atmosphere, ideal for swimming, sun, and water sports. Steps from the hotel.

Swimming · Paddleboarding · Beach Volleyball · Kitesurfing

El Viejo San Juan

A historic colonial district with colorful Spanish architecture, cobblestone streets, and centuries of culture. UNESCO-recognized, minutes away.

Fortresses · Museums · Artisans · Cuisine

Laguna de Condado

A serene coastal lagoon offering calm waters perfect for water sports and wildlife observation, a peaceful escape with stunning views.

Kayaking · Paddleboarding · Wildlife · Waterfront Walks

Museo de Arte de Puerto Rico

A premier cultural institution showcasing Puerto Rican art from the 17th century to contemporary works: permanent collections, rotating exhibitions, a sculpture garden.

Exhibitions · Workshops · Tours · Sculpture Garden

José Celso Barbosa Park

A spacious green park ideal for walks, outdoor workouts, and recreational activities, close to the beach with an open landscape.

Walks · Fitness · Picnics · Playground

Distrito T-Mobile

A vibrant entertainment and dining district featuring restaurants, bars, and live music, perfect for social gatherings and memorable evenings.

Dining · Nightlife · Live Music · Shopping

Gardens, pools and lawn, all the way to the sand.
